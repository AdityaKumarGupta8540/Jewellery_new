The Standards

BIS HALLMARK

All our gold jewellery carries the BIS Hallmark—the ultimate guarantee of gold purity in India. It certifies that the piece meets the standards set by the Bureau of Indian Standards.

HUID

Hallmark Unique Identification (HUID) is a 6-digit alphanumeric code assigned to every piece of jewellery. It ensures traceability and transparency, securing your royal investment.

DIAMOND CERTIFICATION

Our diamonds are certified by reputed independent gemological laboratories (such as IGI or GIA). Each certificate details the 4Cs—Cut, Color, Clarity, and Carat weight.

GEMSTONE & PURITY

Every colored gemstone is ethically sourced and rigorously tested. We provide detailed documentation stating the exact weight and purity of the metals and stones used.
